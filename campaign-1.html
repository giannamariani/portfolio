AKKO Device Insurance

We rely on our phones for so many everyday tasks that, when one breaks, a normal day can feel like stepping back in time. Our campaign makes that feeling tangible by showing everything you would suddenly have to rely on: remembering phone numbers, using a map, and using a separate music player, just to navigate the modern world. Its nostalgic visual style reminds older viewers what life was like before everything was at their fingertips, while showing younger viewers what living without a phone today might actually feel like. AKKO phone insurance gives people a way to prepare for a broken phone and avoid getting stuck jumping through all those extra hoops.
